Standard of Performance.   The Vendor shall perform the Services at professional level,  using its best efforts and at a minimum, to the standard of a reasonable and prudent professional in the same position in this industry. Term of Agreement.  This Agreement shall commence on the date services were first commenced, or the date of signature herein.
